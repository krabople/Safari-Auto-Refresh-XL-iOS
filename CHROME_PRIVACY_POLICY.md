# Privacy Policy for Auto Refresh XL for Chrome

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Auto Refresh XL automatically refreshes user-selected webpages and monitors them for user-defined text conditions. It does not send browsing content, monitoring terms, or settings to the developer or to an external server.

## Local processing and storage

The extension reads the rendered content of webpages to check plain-text, regular-expression, or XPath conditions and to highlight matches, scroll to them, and display alerts. Page content is processed locally in Chrome and is not uploaded or retained as a browsing-content database.

Refresh settings, current timer sessions and deadlines, monitored terms and actions, popup drafts, sound preferences, widget positions, and Auto-Start page URLs or URL patterns are saved in chrome.storage.local on the user's device. These settings may include webpage URLs and text chosen by the user. The extension does not transmit this information to the developer, sell it, or share it with third parties.

## Permissions

HTTP and HTTPS website access supports monitoring and on-page controls on websites selected by the user, saved Auto-Start rules, and detection banners on the currently viewed tab. The storage permission saves settings locally. The alarms permission recovers scheduled refreshes when Chrome suspends the background worker. The notifications permission displays optional desktop alerts. File URLs and browser-internal pages are excluded.

## No tracking or remote service

The extension contains no advertising, analytics, tracking scripts, remote executable code, developer-operated server, account registration, or in-app purchases. It does not collect passwords, payment details, or personal information for the developer. Website requests caused by refreshing a page remain subject to that website's privacy policy and normal browser behavior.

## Your choices

Users can stop timers and monitoring, disable desktop notifications or sounds, and edit or delete Auto-Start rules. Removing the extension removes its local extension storage. Chrome's website-access controls can limit the sites on which the extension runs.
