# Privacy Policy for Auto Refresh XL

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Auto Refresh XL does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

The Safari extension reads the content of webpages only when necessary to provide features requested by the user, such as automatic refreshing, text monitoring, highlighting, and alerts. This processing takes place locally on the user's device. Page content and monitored terms are not transmitted to the developer or to an external server.

Settings such as refresh intervals, monitoring rules, sound preferences, and overlay positions are stored locally by Safari on the user's device. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, accounts, or in-app purchases.

Auto Refresh XL does not operate a developer-controlled server and does not intentionally collect diagnostic or usage information. Websites opened in Safari remain subject to their own privacy policies and data practices.
